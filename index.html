Features

Everything you need to build an AI

From training to chatting to sharing, it all happens in one clean app.

Train your own models

Feed PaneAI your own text and train a model that talks the way you want.

Chat with them

Talk to any of your models in a smooth chat window, just like the big AI apps.

AI that creates files

Ask your model to write something and it saves it as a real file on your PC.

No max length on answers. Your model keeps writing for as long as it needs to.

Export your models

Export any model you trained so you can back it up or share it with friends.

Fast C++ engine

The engine is written in C++, so training and chatting run fast on your own hardware.

FAQ

Questions

Is PaneAI free?

Yes, PaneAI is free to download and use.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You create a model, add your text, and press train. Everything happens through the app.

Where do my models and data go?

Everything you train stays on your own PC. You choose when to export a model and where to put it.

How smart will my model be?

PaneAI trains small models on your own computer. They won't match giant cloud AIs, but they learn from exactly what you give them, and more data and bigger sizes make them better.

Can my AI really make files?

Yes. When you ask your model to write something, PaneAI can save the answer as a file on your PC.
